California-Specific Considerations for San Clemente Sellers

Selling a home in California involves specific legal and financial considerations that differ from other states. As experienced Orange County home buyers, we handle all of these routinely:

  • California disclosure requirements — California sellers must complete extensive disclosure packages. We handle all required disclosures and don't ask you to dig up records you don't have
  • California Tenant Protection Act — If your San Clemente property has tenants, California AB 1482 and local ordinances govern how and when they can be asked to vacate. We take on that responsibility at closing
  • Capital gains considerations — Long-term San Clemente homeowners often have significant appreciation. We can refer you to a 1031 exchange specialist or tax advisor if needed before closing
  • Escrow process — California uses escrow companies rather than attorneys to close transactions. We work with reputable Orange County escrow firms and cover standard closing costs on our end
  • HOA transfer requirements — Many San Clemente communities have HOA transfer fees, required documentation, and approval processes. We navigate these so you don't have to
