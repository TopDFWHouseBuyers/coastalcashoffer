Many Pomona sellers don't realize how much traditional sales actually cost until they're deep in the process. Understanding these expenses helps explain why cash offers often net comparable or better returns.

Standard real estate commissions in California run 5-6% of the sale price. On a $550,000 home (near Pomona's median), that's $27,500-$33,000 in fees before you've addressed any other costs.

Repairs and Staging

Southern California buyers expect move-in ready homes. Even well-maintained properties typically need:

California-Specific Selling Costs

California-Specific Considerations for Pomona Sellers

Selling property in California involves unique legal and financial considerations that impact your transaction.

Disclosure Requirements

California has some of the nation's strictest disclosure laws. Sellers must complete Transfer Disclosure Statements detailing known property issues. While cash buyers still receive these disclosures, they purchase with full awareness of problems that might kill traditional deals.

Property Tax Implications

California's Proposition 13 caps annual property tax increases for current owners, but reassessment occurs upon sale. While this impacts the buyer more than seller, understanding it helps you appreciate why some properties are easier to sell to cash buyers than retail buyers facing dramatically higher tax bills.

Supplemental Tax Bills

California issues supplemental property tax bills after ownership transfers. Ensure your purchase agreement clearly states how these are handled—typically prorated to the closing date.

Title and Escrow

California is an escrow state where neutral third parties manage the closing process. Even in cash sales, working with reputable title and escrow companies protects all parties and ensures clear title transfer.
