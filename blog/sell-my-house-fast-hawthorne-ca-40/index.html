Understanding California's Real Estate Complexities

Selling property in California comes with unique considerations that cash buyers help you navigate seamlessly.

California Tenant Laws and Occupied Properties

If you're selling a Hawthorne rental property with tenants, California's tenant protection laws can complicate traditional sales. The Tenant Protection Act of 2019 provides significant rights to tenants, making it difficult to show properties or require tenant cooperation. Cash buyers purchase occupied properties, allowing tenants to remain through closing and handling any necessary transitions according to California law.

Property Tax Considerations

California's Proposition 13 limits property tax increases for long-term owners, but selling triggers reassessment at current market value. This impacts buyers' calculations but doesn't affect your cash sale proceeds. We factor this into our offers so there are no surprises during the transaction.

Disclosure Requirements

California has some of the nation's strictest seller disclosure requirements. Cash buyers are experienced investors who understand these obligations and purchase properties with full knowledge of potential issues, reducing your liability concerns and simplifying the paperwork process.

Escrow Process in Southern California

California real estate transactions use escrow companies as neutral third parties. Cash sales typically move through escrow much faster than financed purchases because there's no lender involvement. What normally takes 30-45 days can be completed in as little as seven days with cash transactions.
