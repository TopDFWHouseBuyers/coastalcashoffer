The Traditional Home Selling Process vs. Cash Sales

When you list your San Juan Capistrano home the traditional way, you're committing to a lengthy, expensive process that includes:

Traditional Real Estate Sale Timeline

  1. Preparation (2-4 weeks): Making repairs, painting, landscaping, and staging your home
  2. Listing and marketing (1-2 weeks): Professional photos, MLS listing, open houses
  3. Showings (ongoing): Keeping your home spotless for potential buyers, often with little notice
  4. Offer and negotiation (1-2 weeks): Waiting for offers, negotiating terms and price
  5. Escrow and inspections (30-45 days): Buyer inspections, appraisal, financing approval, title work
  6. Potential renegotiation: Buyer requesting repairs or price reductions after inspection
  7. Closing: If everything goes smoothly and financing doesn't fall through

Throughout this process, you'll pay for:

  • Real estate agent commissions (typically 5-6% of sale price)
  • Repairs and updates requested by buyers
  • Staging costs
  • Ongoing mortgage, insurance, and property tax payments
  • HOA fees (if applicable)
  • Utilities and maintenance

California-Specific Considerations When Selling Your Home

Selling property in California comes with unique regulations and market conditions that affect your timeline and process:

Property Disclosure Requirements

California has some of the strictest disclosure laws in the country. Sellers must provide detailed information about property conditions, natural hazards, neighborhood noise, and more. When you sell to a cash buyer, you still need to disclose known issues, but you won't face the same scrutiny or negotiation battles that arise with traditional buyers who might use every disclosed item to request price reductions.

California Escrow Process

California exclusively uses escrow companies (rather than attorneys) to facilitate real estate transactions. While this system works well, it adds time to traditional sales. Cash transactions move through escrow much faster because there's no lender involvement, no appraisal contingencies, and fewer parties coordinating documentation.

Tenant-Occupied Properties

If you're selling a rental property in San Juan Capistrano, California's tenant-friendly laws can complicate traditional sales. The state provides significant protections for renters, including strict eviction procedures and notice requirements. Many traditional buyers won't purchase tenant-occupied properties, but cash buyers regularly do, allowing you to sell without navigating the complex eviction process.

Property Tax Considerations

Thanks to Proposition 13, California property taxes are based on purchase price rather than current market value. This means your property taxes may be significantly lower than what a new buyer would pay. When working with cash buyers, you can discuss how to handle prorated property taxes clearly and transparently during the closing process.
