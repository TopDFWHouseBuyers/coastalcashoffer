California-Specific Considerations When Selling

Selling real estate in California involves unique requirements that can complicate traditional sales but are easily managed in cash transactions:

Disclosure Requirements

California law requires extensive property disclosures, including the Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS), Natural Hazard Disclosure (NHD), and specific disclosures about earthquake safety, environmental hazards, and property defects. While these requirements still apply in cash sales, experienced cash buyers are familiar with California properties and aren't typically fazed by disclosure items that might scare off traditional buyers.

Supplemental Property Tax

California's supplemental property tax system can surprise sellers. When property changes hands, the new assessed value generates a supplemental tax bill. In cash transactions, we clearly outline who pays what, so there are no surprises after closing.

Capital Gains Considerations

With Redondo Beach's appreciated property values, many sellers have substantial capital gains. While we're not tax advisors, we recommend consulting with a California CPA about the primary residence exclusion (up to $250,000 for individuals, $500,000 for couples) and other strategies to minimize your tax burden.

HOA Requirements

Many Redondo Beach properties are part of homeowners associations with specific transfer requirements. Cash buyers experienced in Southern California real estate handle HOA documentation routinely, ensuring smooth compliance with all association rules.
