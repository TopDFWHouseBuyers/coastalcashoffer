California-Specific Considerations

Selling a house in California comes with unique factors that don't apply in other states. Property taxes in Orange County average 1.1% of assessed value, and Proposition 19 has changed inheritance rules for property tax reassessment. California's disclosure requirements are among the strictest in the nation, requiring sellers to reveal known defects and issues. The escrow process involves specific California regulations and timelines that can extend closing dates.

Additionally, California tenant protection laws are some of the strongest in the country. If you have a rental property with tenants in place, eviction can take months and cost thousands in legal fees and lost rent. These complications make cash sales particularly attractive for Buena Park landlords.
