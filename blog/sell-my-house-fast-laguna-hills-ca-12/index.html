California Legal and Tax Considerations

Selling real estate in California involves specific legal requirements and potential tax implications that homeowners should understand.

Mandatory Disclosures

California requires sellers to complete a Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) revealing known property defects. Even in as-is cash sales, sellers must disclose material facts about the property's condition. Golden Coast Cash Offer's streamlined process makes these disclosures simple, and we won't use them to renegotiate or back out of the deal.

Property Tax Considerations

Under Proposition 13, California property taxes are based on purchase price. When you sell, supplemental property tax adjustments may occur. Your escrow company will prorate property taxes through closing, ensuring you only pay for the time you owned the property.

Capital Gains Exclusions

Many Laguna Hills homeowners qualify for significant capital gains tax exclusions. The IRS allows up to $250,000 in capital gains exclusion for single filers and $500,000 for married couples filing jointly on primary residence sales, provided you've lived in the home for at least two of the past five years. Consult with a tax professional about your specific situation.
