California-Specific Selling Considerations

Selling property in California involves unique factors that don't apply in other states:

  • Property Tax Implications: Under Proposition 19, property tax considerations have changed for inherited properties and homeowners over 55
  • Disclosure Requirements: California has extensive mandatory disclosure requirements, though cash sales often involve fewer contingencies
  • Escrow Process: California uses an escrow system rather than attorney closings, typically taking 30-60 days for traditional sales but much faster for cash transactions
  • Transfer Taxes: Orange County and Santa Ana impose transfer taxes on property sales, calculated as a percentage of the sale price
