California-Specific Considerations When Selling Your Costa Mesa Home

Selling property in California comes with unique considerations that don't apply in other states. Understanding these factors helps explain why many Costa Mesa homeowners prefer cash sales:

California Tenant Protection Laws

If you have tenants in your Costa Mesa property, California's tenant-friendly laws can complicate a traditional sale. The Tenant Protection Act of 2019 provides significant protections for renters, making it difficult to sell occupied rental properties through conventional means. Cash buyers like Golden Coast Cash Offer purchase properties with tenants in place, eliminating this complication entirely.

Prop 19 and Property Tax Implications

California's Proposition 19, which took effect in 2021, changed the rules for property tax transfers and inherited properties. If you've inherited a Costa Mesa home and don't plan to use it as your primary residence, you may face significant property tax reassessments. Selling quickly for cash can help you avoid ongoing tax obligations on a property you don't want to keep.

California Disclosure Requirements

California has extensive disclosure requirements for home sellers, including the Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) and Natural Hazard Disclosure Statement. While these protect buyers, they add complexity to traditional sales. Cash buyers are typically experienced investors who purchase properties as-is, streamlining the disclosure process.

High Transaction Costs

With Costa Mesa's median home prices consistently above the national average, the costs of traditional selling add up quickly. A $900,000 home sold through a traditional agent incurs approximately $54,000 in commissions alone, plus closing costs, potential repair expenses, and carrying costs during the selling period. Cash sales eliminate agent commissions entirely.
