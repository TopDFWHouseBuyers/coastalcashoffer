Understanding California's Real Estate Challenges

Selling property in California comes with unique considerations that can complicate traditional sales. Understanding these factors helps explain why many Mission Viejo homeowners opt for direct cash sales instead.

California Tenant Laws and Rental Properties

If you own a rental property in Mission Viejo with tenants, California's robust tenant protection laws can make selling complicated. The state's laws heavily favor tenants, requiring specific notice periods and often allowing tenants to remain during the sales process. Many traditional buyers won't even consider occupied properties or those with problematic tenants.

Cash buyers like Golden Coast Cash Offer regularly purchase tenant-occupied properties. We handle the complexities of California tenant law, and you don't have to worry about eviction proceedings or vacant the property before closing.

Escrow Process in Southern California

California uses an escrow-based closing system that typically takes 30-45 days for traditional sales—sometimes longer if there are financing contingencies, inspection issues, or title problems. Each delay costs you time and money in mortgage payments, property taxes, insurance, and HOA fees (common in Mission Viejo's planned communities).

With a cash sale, we can close in as little as 7 days, or on your preferred timeline. There's no waiting for buyer financing approval, no appraisal contingencies, and significantly less paperwork. The escrow process becomes straightforward when cash buyers are involved.

Property Taxes and Proposition 19

California's recent changes to property tax inheritance rules under Proposition 19 have created urgency for some families dealing with inherited homes. If you've inherited a Mission Viejo property and don't plan to use it as your primary residence, you may face significant property tax reassessment. Selling quickly to a cash buyer can help you avoid ongoing tax burdens and maintenance costs on a property you don't need.
