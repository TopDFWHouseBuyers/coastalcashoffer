California-Specific Considerations When Selling Rental Properties

Tenant Rights and Notifications

California law requires landlords to provide proper notice to tenants when selling a property. However, when you sell to a cash buyer who purchases with tenants in place, you avoid many of the complications associated with showings, open houses, and coordinating access with tenants who may not be cooperative.

Property Tax Implications

Selling investment property in California triggers capital gains taxes on your profit. The amount depends on how long you've owned the property, your tax bracket, and whether you're using strategies like a 1031 exchange to defer taxes. While Golden Coast Cash Offer doesn't provide tax advice, we recommend consulting with a CPA or tax advisor to understand your specific situation.

Escrow Process

California real estate transactions require an escrow process managed by a neutral third party. We work with experienced local escrow companies familiar with Orange County transactions, ensuring all paperwork is handled correctly and your interests are protected throughout the sale.
