Understanding Probate in California

Before you can sell an inherited property in California, you'll typically need to navigate the probate process. This legal procedure validates the will and transfers ownership to heirs. In Los Angeles County, where Compton is located, probate can take 9-18 months depending on the complexity of the estate.

Once you receive clear title through probate, you're free to sell the property. Some inherited homes can be sold during probate with court approval, which can actually speed up the process if you're working with a cash buyer who can close quickly.

California Property Tax Considerations

California's Proposition 19, which took effect in 2021, changed the rules for inherited properties. Unless you plan to use the home as your primary residence, you'll likely face a property tax reassessment at current market value. This can significantly increase your annual tax burden, making a quick sale more financially appealing.

Step-by-Step: Selling Your Inherited Compton Home

California-Specific Considerations for Inherited Properties

California's real estate laws include unique requirements that affect inherited property sales:

  • Transfer tax: Los Angeles County charges transfer taxes on property sales, which vary by jurisdiction
  • Natural Hazard Disclosure: Required for all California home sales, disclosing fire, flood, and earthquake zones
  • Escrow requirements: California uses escrow companies to facilitate closings, typically taking 30-45 days for traditional sales (but just 7-14 days with cash buyers)
  • Title insurance: Essential in California to protect against title defects that may have existed before you inherited the property
