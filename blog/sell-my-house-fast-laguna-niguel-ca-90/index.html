California-Specific Considerations When Selling Your Home

Dealing with Tenants in California

If you're selling a rental property in Laguna Niguel, California's tenant protection laws can complicate traditional sales. The state's strong renter protections mean you may need to wait for leases to expire or navigate complex eviction procedures. Cash buyers can purchase occupied properties, often allowing tenants to stay through the transition or working within legal guidelines to resolve the situation fairly.

Property Tax Implications

California's Proposition 13 caps annual property tax increases for current owners, but selling triggers a reassessment. Understanding your tax basis and potential capital gains is important. While we can't provide tax advice, we can work with your schedule to help you time your sale appropriately.

Escrow Process in Southern California

California is an escrow state, meaning a neutral third party handles the transaction. While traditional sales often take 30-60 days to close escrow, cash sales can close in as little as seven days because there's no lender involvement or appraisal contingency.
