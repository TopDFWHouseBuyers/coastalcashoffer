California-Specific Considerations When Selling Your Tustin Home

California Tenant Laws and Occupied Properties

If you own a rental property in Tustin with tenants, California's tenant-friendly laws can complicate a traditional sale. The state requires specific notice periods, and many conventional buyers don't want to deal with occupied properties. Cash buyers like Golden Coast Cash Offer regularly purchase occupied rental properties, handling all tenant situations legally and professionally.
