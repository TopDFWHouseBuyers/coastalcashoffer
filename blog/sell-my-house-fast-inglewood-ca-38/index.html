California-Specific Considerations When Selling Rental Property

Selling rental property in California involves unique considerations that out-of-state investors or inexperienced sellers often underestimate.

Understanding Your Tax Implications

California landlords face both federal and state capital gains taxes when selling rental property. Depending on your situation, you may benefit from a 1031 exchange or other tax strategies. While we're not tax advisors, working with a cash buyer gives you the certainty of a closing date and sale price needed to plan effectively with your CPA.

Navigating California Tenant Laws

California's tenant protection laws are among the strictest in the nation. The statewide Tenant Protection Act limits your ability to terminate tenancies, and Los Angeles County has additional protections. Selling to a cash buyer who understands these laws eliminates your need to navigate eviction proceedings or just-cause termination requirements.

Disclosure Requirements

California requires extensive property disclosures, and rental properties come with additional considerations around lead paint, mold, and habitability issues. Cash buyers are experienced investors who understand these requirements and accept properties with known issues.
