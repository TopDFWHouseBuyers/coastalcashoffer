Understanding California's Real Estate Requirements

Disclosure Laws in California

California has strict disclosure requirements for home sales. Sellers must complete a Transfer Disclosure Statement revealing known issues with the property. While cash buyers purchase homes as-is, you still have disclosure obligations. However, the process is streamlined—you're not trying to hide issues from buyers who might back out; instead, you're working with a cash buyer who factors everything into their offer upfront.

Navigating Tenant Situations in Orange

If you're selling a rental property in Orange with existing tenants, California's tenant protection laws can complicate a traditional sale. The state's rent control measures and just-cause eviction requirements make it challenging to remove tenants, even when selling. Many traditional buyers won't purchase occupied properties or will demand significant price reductions.

Cash buyers like Golden Coast Cash Offer regularly purchase tenant-occupied properties. We can close with tenants in place, work with them on relocation, or structure the sale to address your specific situation without violating California tenant laws.

Property Taxes and Proposition 19

California homeowners should be aware of Proposition 19, which changed property tax inheritance rules in 2021. If you've inherited property in Orange and don't plan to make it your primary residence, you may face property tax reassessment. Selling quickly for cash can help you avoid ongoing tax increases and maintenance costs on an inherited home you don't intend to keep.
